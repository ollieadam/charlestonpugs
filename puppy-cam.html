Puppy Cam

Barbie & her three puppies, live from the pen in North Charleston.

This is the live puppy cam for Barbie the Pug (fawn Pug, Dermit line) and her three puppies, born November 12–13, 2026, here in North Charleston, South Carolina. The camera looks right into the puppy pen, so you can check in on Barbie and the pups as they eat, sleep, and start to explore.

The cam is on-demand: it only runs while someone is actually watching, and it rests when nobody is — so if the window below says the cam is off, it just means nobody has it on right now. When people do watch, the visit is saved, and you can replay earlier visits in the same window.
